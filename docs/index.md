# The Plain White T-Shirt

*A small object. A surprisingly big world of ideas.*

Put a plain white T-shirt on a table. It has no slogan to explain itself, no graphic to borrow a personality from. Now imagine presenting that same shirt as an invitation to explore, a carefully considered essential, or a small act of rebellion.

The shirt stays put. The meaning moves.

This book explores how that happens—and how to direct AI with enough vocabulary and intention to shape what it makes. Persuasion gives us a language for response. Archetypes give us a language for identity. Design gives those ideas a visible form.

Think of AI's first draft as a block of material. You decide what the work is becoming. Each prompt makes a deliberate cut: keep this, remove that, sharpen the relationship between the two.

> **An open manuscript:** this is the book's starting structure. The chapters below are short outlines, ready to grow through research, examples, and conversation.
