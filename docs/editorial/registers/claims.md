## Rules

- A source supports a specific claim, not every sentence near it.
- A company’s statement is evidence of what the company says; it is not independent proof of performance.
- A personal recollection is a first-person account; do not silently convert it into a general fact.
- An invented product fact is useful for a controlled demonstration only when labeled.
- A candidate source remains a lead until someone inspects the linked material and records a locator.
