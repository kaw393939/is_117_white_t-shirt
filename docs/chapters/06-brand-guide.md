# A brand someone else can build

> Chapter outline · ready to develop.

You hand your idea to another person. They ask: “Which blue? How much space? What does confident sound like?” A guide turns a private picture in your head into decisions another person can use.

## The chapter ahead

- Brand strategy: audience, promise, positioning, and voice.
- Visual style: typography, color, spacing, imagery, and layout.
- Examples that show a rule working—and show where it stops.
- A shared vocabulary for discussing changes with a developer.
- A handoff that leaves room for collaboration without leaving everything to guesswork.

## A useful reference

The [U.S. Web Design System's design tokens](https://designsystem.digital.gov/design-tokens/) show how repeated style decisions can become named values. We can borrow that principle without borrowing its visual identity or technical stack.
