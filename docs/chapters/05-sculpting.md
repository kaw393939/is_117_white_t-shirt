# Sculpting with AI

> Chapter outline · ready to develop.

“Make it better” hands the decision back to the machine. “Keep the Explorer story, remove the luxury cues, and make the headline sound curious rather than boastful” begins to shape the material.

## The chapter ahead

- Start with an intention and generate contrasting directions.
- Name what works before requesting a change.
- Use the vocabulary of persuasion, archetypes, and design as precise instructions.
- Change a manageable part, inspect the result, and decide what to keep.
- Preserve useful versions so exploration does not erase good work.

> Keep the product facts and Explorer direction. Rewrite only the headline and opening paragraph. Replace status language with everyday curiosity. Give me three alternatives. Do not add product claims.
