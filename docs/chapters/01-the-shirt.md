# The shirt stays. The story changes.

> Chapter outline · ready to develop.

Imagine three shop windows. The same white shirt appears in each. One is spare and orderly. One looks like a travel journal. One seems to be arguing with the idea of advertising itself.

These are invented scenes. Our question is real: what can presentation change when the product stays the same?

## The chapter ahead

- Introduce the three lenses: persuasion, archetype, and visual language.
- Separate a product fact from a story around it.
- Follow one shirt through several meanings.
- Introduce the reader as the person directing the work.

## A thought to start with

Describe the same shirt as practical, adventurous, and rebellious. What changed in your language? What stayed true about the shirt?
