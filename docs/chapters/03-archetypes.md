# Who do you get to be?

> Chapter outline · ready to develop.

One shirt whispers “fit in.” Another says “find your own way.” Same shirt; different invitation. An archetype gives us a name for a recognizable story, then lets us ask whether our words and images actually tell it.

## The chapter ahead

- Explorer, Sage, Creator, Rebel, and the wider archetype vocabulary.
- Identity as an invitation, not a guaranteed transformation.
- Combining tendencies without turning people into fixed types.
- Cultural context and the gap between intended and received meaning.

## Begin at the source

[Carol S. Pearson explains her archetype system](https://carolspearson.com/about/the-pearson-12-archetype-system-human-development-and-evolution), including how names change across applications. Read it as the theorist's account of a model, not a literal diagnosis of every customer.
