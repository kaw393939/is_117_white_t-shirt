# Dossier: Blank Canvas Co.

> A complete worked example. Blank Canvas Co. is an **invented brand** created for this book. Same fictional shirt as [Standard Issue](standard-issue.md) and [Drift & Thread](drift-thread.md) — told by the class clown.

## Archetype card

- **Primary archetype: Jester.** The shirt that refuses to take the transaction seriously. Joy as the value proposition; the joke is the invitation.
- **Accent: a dab of Creator.** A white tee *is* literally a blank canvas — the brand knows it and won't stop saying it.
- **Failure mode to avoid:** noise without warmth. A joke that laughs *at* the customer ends the relationship. Every gag must land on the brand's side of the transaction — the product stays honestly described even while everything around it clowns.
- **Persona sketch (invented):** someone who finds most product pages faintly ridiculous and rewards a brand that agrees; shares screenshots of funny copy; will absolutely read the FAQ for entertainment.

## Voice sheet

1. The joke leads; the fact follows immediately. Never sacrifice accuracy for the bit.
2. Self-aware about selling: this page knows it is an ad, and says so.
3. Puns permitted. Exclamation points rationed: one per page, maximum.
4. Punch up at marketing, never down at the buyer.
5. The facts section drops the act completely. Trust is the straight man.

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| "A white T-shirt. Revolutionary. Nobody has ever thought of this." | "LOL buy this NOW random shopper!!" |
| "One hundred percent cotton. We checked. Twice." | "This shirt will change your LIFE (jk… unless?)" |
| "Machine wash cold. It has been through enough." | "Washing instructions? Figure it out, champ." |

- **Type:** deliberately mixed — a heavy grotesque headline colliding with a plain text face. Rotated labels and sticker-style elements allowed. This is the postmodern answer to Standard Issue's Swiss grid, on purpose.
- **Layout:** the grid exists and is broken on purpose — tilted cards, overlapping stickers, one element per view escaping its column. Chaos with a backstage pass: the buying path itself stays perfectly straight.
- **Imagery:** the shirt doing things shirts don't do — worn as a chef's hat, framed in a museum, presented on a pillow like jewelry. Product shots stay honest; the staging does the joking.

## Product copy

**Hero line:** It's a white T-shirt. We've made peace with that.

**Description (60 words):**

> Behold: a shirt. White. Short sleeves. A hole for your head — arguably our best feature. One hundred percent cotton, 180 grams per square meter, which is a real number we are proud of. It will not change your life. It will cover your torso competently and without ceremony. Honestly, for $28, that's a steal.

**Facts (identical product, invented for the example):** 100% cotton, 180 gsm · straight cut, crew neck, pre-shrunk · machine wash cold · cut and sewn in Portugal · $28.
