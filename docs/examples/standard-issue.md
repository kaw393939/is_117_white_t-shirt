# Dossier: Standard Issue

> A complete worked example. Standard Issue is an **invented brand** created for this book. Every artifact below — the voice rules, the palette, the copy — is original to this project and freely reusable as a pattern.

This dossier gathers the decisions scattered through the chapters into one place: the archetype card, the voice sheet, the style tile, and the product copy. Its final artifact is the [Standard Issue storefront](storefront.md). Use it as the reference shape for your own brand.

## Archetype card

- **Primary archetype: Everyperson.** The dependable regular. Belonging through honesty, not exclusivity. The shirt as the end of decision-making.
- **Accent: a trace of Sage.** Enough knowledge to be trusted on fabric and construction — never enough to lecture.
- **Failure mode to avoid:** blandness. Dependable curdles into forgettable if the voice loses its dry edge. The antidote is specificity: real numbers, real details, quiet confidence.
- **Persona sketch (invented):** someone who owns three white tees and wants to own the *right* one; reads reviews for durability, not status; is annoyed by hype and timers.

## Voice sheet

Five rules a stranger could follow:

1. Declarative sentences. Short ones.
2. Concrete nouns: fiber, seam, gram, wash. No "journey," no "elevated."
3. Dry humor is welcome. Sarcasm is not.
4. No urgency, ever. No timers, no "almost gone," no exclamation points.
5. Product facts only in the facts section. The story stays out of the spec sheet.

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| "One good basic. No decisions." | "Your wardrobe journey starts here!" |
| "180 gsm. Heavy enough to not be see-through." | "Luxurious, premium fabric feel." |
| "Buy it once or buy it every year." | "Last chance to transform your style." |

- **Type:** one grotesque sans (system stack), two weights. Headings may set in a serif for warmth, as this book does — but never in all caps, never condensed, never playful.
- **Layout:** strict grid, generous margins, one accent element per view. Swiss restraint as the message.
- **Imagery:** single garment, flat daylight, no props, no models' faces.

## Product copy

**Hero line:** One good basic. No decisions.

**Description (60 words):**

> A plain white T-shirt in midweight cotton. We make one, we make it properly, and we keep making it. The knit is heavy enough to wear on its own, soft enough to sleep in, and cut straight so it works under anything. Buy it once or buy it every year. It will be the same shirt.

**Facts (invented for the example):** 100% cotton, 180 gsm · straight cut, crew neck, pre-shrunk · machine wash cold · cut and sewn in Portugal · $28.
